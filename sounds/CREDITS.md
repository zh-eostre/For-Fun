# 音效来源 / Sound credits

The crowd sounds are short clips cut from these recordings: mixed to mono, resampled to 22.05 kHz, trimmed, faded in
and out, and set to an even volume.

| File | Source | Author | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `goal.wav` | "Crowd Cheering Sounds – 04 – Strong cheering – II – Short", from [Free Crowd Cheering Sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/free-crowd-cheering-sounds) | Gregor Quendel | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| `roar.wav` | "Crowd Cheering Sounds – 03 – Strong cheering – I", from [Free Crowd Cheering Sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/free-crowd-cheering-sounds) | Gregor Quendel | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| `concede.wav` | ["crowd `oh - - disappointed"](https://freesound.org/people/mrrap4food/sounds/619007/) | mrrap4food | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |

The other effects (whistles, card beeps, the signature-move whoosh, the fanfare) are synthesised in `js/sound.js`.
